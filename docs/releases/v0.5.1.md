# v0.5.1 — tofu-free forky glyphs, greeter power & cursor polish, and 4-target ARM64 release

A focused maintenance and milestone release building on `v0.5.0`: every TUI
icon, notification glyph, multi-field cursor, and console power control works
cleanly on live Debian `forky/arm64` hardware, while four release images
(`google-asurada`, `google-kukui`, `arm64-uefi`, `arm64-extlinux`) bring LazyOS
not only to Google Hayato (`MT8192`) and Kukui/Duet (`MT8183`) Chromebooks,
but also to Raspberry Pi 4/5 (via EDK2 UEFI or U-Boot), ARM64 UEFI laptops/VMs,
and U-Boot Single-Board Computers.

## What changed since v0.5.0

### Tofu-free icon rendering on Debian `forky` (`3.4.0+dfsg2-1`)

- **Remapped all 73 stripped Codicon codepoints (`U+EA60..U+EC1E`)**: Debian `forky`
  upgraded `fonts-nerd-symbols` from `3.4.0+dfsg1-2` to `3.4.0+dfsg2-1`, which
  removed Codicons while retaining Octicons, Devicons, Seti, Powerline, and IEC.
  [`make-glyphs.sh`](../../desktop/scripts/make-glyphs.sh) now maps all **101**
  UI `Glyph` variants to non-shadowed BMP codepoints covered by both `forky`'s
  `Symbols Nerd Font Mono` and `Font Awesome 6 Free Solid` / `Brands Regular`
  (`101 drawn, 0 text fallbacks`), and `foot.ini.tpl` includes
  `Font Awesome 6 Brands Regular:size=12` alongside Solid.
- **Full symbol fallback in `lazyos-notify`**:
  `FontRenderer` now loads `fa-6.7.2-solid-900.ttf` and `fa-6.7.2-brands-400.ttf`
  after `SymbolsNerdFontMono-Regular.ttf`, and notification/OSD cards resolve
  icons from `lazyos_ui::Glyph` plus Octicon mute (`U+F466`) and Font Awesome
  close (`U+F00D`) instead of hardcoded Codicon escapes.
- **Resilient glyph verification in `check-desktop.sh`**:
  `30-desktop.sh` verifies every `lazyos --glyphs` codepoint against installed
  font charsets first and tolerates `dfsg1`/`dfsg2` build-pin hash differences
  when 100% of codepoints are present, failing with the exact missing codepoints
  if a future font update drops coverage.

### Greeter & installer UI/UX polish

- **Blinking focus-aware cursors & accessible contrast**:
  Added shared `Input::render_field(..., focused, blink_on, bg_role, ...)`
  across `InstallerScreen`, `GreeterScreen`, and `GreeterSettingsScreen`: only
  the focused input shows the accent bar and 500 ms blinking cursor (`solid` when
  `LAZYOS_REDUCED_MOTION=1`), `ListState` fills selection rows uniformly with a
  2-space glyph gap, and `+ Other` / input boxes use `ColorRole::Bg` inside
  `ColorRole::Surface` cards for Solarized Dark contrast.
- **Installer `80x24` responsiveness, header readability & ChromeOS safety polish**:
  Compact responsive layouts at `80x24`, single-line breadcrumbs without
  overlap, mouse hit/click support on list rows and buttons, higher-contrast
  header titles, sysfs `type`/`scope` power detection for ChromeOS `USB` PD
  chargers & SBS batteries (ignoring stylus/HID `Device` batteries),
  `check_machine_safety` enforcement in headless `apply`, dynamic
  `deviceinfo` partition diagram sizing, and Depthcharge boot instructions
  covering both `"internal disk"` menus and legacy `Ctrl+D`.
- **Clean console power actions in `lazyos-greeter`**:
  `GreeterScreen::execute_power` now passes `-i` to `systemctl`, redirects
  `stdin`/`stdout`/`stderr` so systemd inhibitor notices never corrupt the raw
  Ratatui terminal, formats error details cleanly without duplicate `exit `
  prefixes, and ships `/etc/polkit-1/rules.d/49-lazyos-greeter-power.rules`
  authorizing local active `_greetd`/`greetd` Sleep / Reboot / Shut down actions.

### Google Kukui (MT8183) Chromebook Family & Target-Direct Home Profiles

- **New target `google-kukui` (`devices/google-kukui/`)**: Adds Depthcharge image
  support for the MediaTek MT8183 Chromebook family (Lenovo IdeaPad Duet
  `krane`, `kodama`, `kakadu`, `katsu`, and `jacuzzi-*` variants) sharing our
  `7.2.7-mt81` kernel (`mediatek-mt81`), `firmware-atheros` + `firmware-mediatek`,
  QCA6174 BT I2S NVM & `mt8183_da7219_m` ALSA UCM2 symlinks, Google Hammer
  detachable keyboard `keyd` bindings, `11230000.mmc` / `11240000.mmc` IRQ
  affinity, and `DSI-1` internal panel handling in `lazyos`.
- **Developer menu home-folder backup & target-direct streaming**:
  `lazyos-profile` / Developer menu supports optional `--include-home` backups
  and streams archives directly to the target drive without staging multi-GB
  tarballs on the local root filesystem.

### Generic ARM64 Reach (`arm64-uefi` & `arm64-extlinux`)

Alongside our dedicated Chromebook builds, two generic `forky/arm64` targets
open LazyOS up to a wide range of ARM64 hardware using Debian's mainline
`linux-image-arm64` (`7.2.15+deb14-arm64`), synced Devicetree bundles, automatic
`apt upgrade` kernel refresh (`/etc/kernel/postinst.d/zz-lazyos-boot`), and
broad multi-vendor firmware (`firmware-brcm80211`, `firmware-qcom-soc`,
`firmware-atheros`, `firmware-iwlwifi`, `firmware-mediatek`, `firmware-realtek`,
`firmware-linux-free`, `firmware-linux-nonfree`, `firmware-misc-nonfree`):

- **`arm64-uefi.img.xz` (UEFI Systems, VMs & EDK2 Boards)**: Ships a 256 MiB
  `FAT32` ESP with `systemd-boot` (`BOOTAA64.EFI`) and full `/boot/efi/dtb/`
  support—ready for ACPI/EBBR ARM64 laptops and workstations, QEMU `virt` /
  UTM / Parallels virtual machines, and **Raspberry Pi 4 / Pi 5** boards running
  EDK2/Tianocore UEFI firmware (or U-Boot EFI).
- **`arm64-extlinux.img.xz` (U-Boot / Devicetree SBCs)**: Ships a 512 MiB
  `ext2` `/boot` starting at `16 MiB` with `/boot/extlinux/extlinux.conf`
  (`FDTDIR /dtbs/`), targeting U-Boot Single-Board Computers (Rockchip,
  Allwinner, Amlogic, Raspberry Pi U-Boot, etc.). Boards without U-Boot in
  SPI/eMMC can flash their board's U-Boot SPL into the reserved `1–16 MiB`
  pre-partition gap after `dd`.

## What it does not carry

Release artifacts are sanitized and asserted clean by
[`scripts/lib-dist.sh`](../../core/scripts/lib-dist.sh): no Wi-Fi profiles, no
SSH host keys, no `machine-id` (regenerated on first boot), and no personal files
beyond the default session configuration.

## Known limitations

- **`lazyos-notify` is resident** for the session next to `cliphist` and
  `workstyle` to own `org.freedesktop.Notifications`; it idles without redrawing.
- **MT8192 external display gamma**: OVL1→RDMA1→DPI has no gamma block bound, so
  `wlsunset` applies night light on the built-in panel only.
- **Wayvnc is loopback-only and read-only** (`lazyos menu vnc` prints the SSH
  tunnel command).
- **Camera portal & Xwayland gestures**: Flatpak camera portal is absent (native
  browsers work), and three-finger Firefox tab swipes require native Wayland.
- **Fixed image sizes**: `google-asurada` / `google-kukui` (`6144 MiB`) and mainline ARM64 images
  (`8192 MiB`) leave extra card space unallocated unless written via
  `scripts/write-sd.sh`.
- **Generic ARM64 board firmware/SPL**: `arm64-extlinux.img.xz` intentionally leaves
  the `1–16 MiB` pre-partition gap empty (SBCs without SPI/eMMC U-Boot must flash
  their board U-Boot/SPL there), and Raspberry Pi 4/5 requires RPi EDK2 UEFI firmware
  (`arm64-uefi`) or U-Boot (`arm64-extlinux`).
- **MT81 GPU codecs**: No hardware video decoder, and Panfrost GL/EGL handles
  compositing (PanVK does not support Mali-G57/G72).
